## Install on your Android phone

1. Open the download link on your phone and download `kinnect-0.1.0.apk`.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install this app, then tap **Install**.
3. Open **Kinnect**, tap **Demo Lab**, and choose a parent or child. Try **One month**, **One year**, **Mixed progress**, **Almost legendary**, **Legendary unlocked**, or enter custom streak counts.

Android 7.0 or newer is required. This is an early test edition, outside the Play Store. There is no automatic updater yet; future versions are installed from their release links.

## What this test includes

Parent and child views, private parent incentives, shared achievements, reward balances and goals, streaks and animated animals, agendas, grade-aware school PDF review, and optional candle-lighting/Havdalah times. Havdalah defaults to 42 minutes after shkiah; the Family Organizer controls timing offsets. Personal location and Ashkenaz/Sfard preferences can be changed from **Agenda → Time settings**.

**Demo Lab needs no account. Its sample data stays on each device. Real family accounts, cross-phone syncing, and remote parent-to-child alerts are not enabled yet.** The browser demo and Android app have separate local demo data. Live candle-time lookup requires internet access. Actual retailer prices are entered manually, purchases are disabled, and location updates are explicit rather than continuous background tracking.
